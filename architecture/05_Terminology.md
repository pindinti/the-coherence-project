# Terminology

## Purpose

This document defines the official terminology used throughout The Coherence Project.

Its objective is to ensure conceptual consistency across every publication, language and medium.

Whenever a concept has an official definition, that definition takes precedence over synonyms or stylistic variations.

---

# Terminology Principles

- One concept should have one preferred term.
- Definitions should evolve only through deliberate revision.
- Translations should preserve concepts rather than literal wording.
- Consistency is more important than variety.

---

# Core Concepts

## Organizational Coherence

**Definition**

The dynamic alignment between an organization's promise and its actual capability to consistently deliver that promise.

**Portuguese**

Coerência Organizacional

**Abbreviation**

OC

**Notes**

This is the central concept of the project.

---

## Promise

**Definition**

The explicit or implicit expectation created in the minds of customers, employees, investors or society regarding what an organization consistently delivers.

**Portuguese**

Promessa

---

## Capability

**Definition**

The combination of people, processes, knowledge, technology, culture and resources that enables an organization to fulfill its promise.

**Portuguese**

Capacidade

---

## Alignment

**Definition**

The degree of consistency between different organizational elements.

Alignment contributes to coherence but is not equivalent to Organizational Coherence.

**Portuguese**

Alinhamento

---

## Principle

**Definition**

A fundamental statement that remains valid across different contexts.

**Portuguese**

Princípio

---

## Framework

**Definition**

A structured model used to understand or apply concepts (e.g. an Organizational Coherence framework).

**Portuguese**

Framework

**Disambiguation**

This is the **conceptual/theoretical** use of the term. It is distinct from the **technical framework** (Astro) referenced in 07.2_Framework_and_Platform_Decision, which is a software tool, not an OC concept. Context makes the meaning clear in practice; this note exists only to prevent confusion when both documents are read together (e.g. by an AI assistant implementing the project).

# Reserved Terms

The following concepts should always use their official names.

| Official Term | Avoid |
|--------------|-------|
| Organizational Coherence | Business Alignment |
| Organizational Coherence | Strategic Alignment |
| Promise | Value Proposition (when referring to the central concept) |
| Capability | Competence (unless intentionally distinguished) |

# Revision Policy

Changes to terminology should be rare.

When a definition changes, every affected publication should eventually be updated to preserve conceptual consistency.
