# Product Principles

## Purpose

These principles guide every decision related to The Coherence Project.

Whenever a design, content or technical decision is uncertain, these principles take precedence over personal preferences or temporary trends.

---

# Principle 1 — Knowledge Is the Product

The primary product is knowledge.

The website, articles, podcast, book and every other medium exist only to make that knowledge accessible.

---

# Principle 2 — Clarity Over Complexity

Every decision should improve understanding.

Complexity is acceptable only when it serves clarity.

The project should be intellectually rigorous without becoming intellectually inaccessible.

---

# Principle 3 — Content Before Design

Design exists to support reading, not to compete with it.

Visual elements should improve comprehension rather than attract attention.

The reading experience always comes first.

---

# Principle 4 — Theory Must Meet Practice

Ideas become valuable only when tested.

The project continuously connects theoretical development with practical application through real-world experimentation.

---

# Principle 5 — Build in Public

Whenever appropriate, research, experiments, decisions and lessons learned should be documented publicly.

Transparency increases credibility.

---

# Principle 6 — Evergreen Before Trending

The project prioritizes ideas that remain valuable over time.

Publishing frequency is less important than long-term relevance.

---

# Principle 7 — Simplicity Scales

Simple structures are easier to understand, maintain and evolve.

Every new feature should reduce friction rather than introduce complexity.

---

# Principle 8 — Bilingual by Design

English and Portuguese are first-class languages within the project.

Neither language is considered secondary.

Content should be written or adapted with each audience in mind rather than mechanically translated.

---

# Principle 9 — Consistency Builds Trust

Terminology, visual language and conceptual definitions should remain consistent across every publication.

Readers should recognize the project's identity regardless of the medium.

---

# Principle 10 — Evidence Before Opinion

Arguments should be supported by reasoning, observation or practical experience whenever possible.

The project values intellectual honesty over certainty.

---

# Principle 11 — Continuous Evolution

Every document, article and framework may evolve as understanding improves.

Versioning is encouraged.

Ideas are refined through learning, not protected from revision.

---

# Principle 12 — Long-Term Thinking

The Coherence Project is intended to become a lasting body of work.

Short-term opportunities should never compromise long-term credibility.

---

# Decision Filter

Before implementing any significant change, ask:

- Does this improve understanding?
- Does this strengthen the project's credibility?
- Does this support the long-term vision?
- Does this respect the project's principles?
- Would this decision still make sense five years from now?

If the answer to most of these questions is "no", the change should be reconsidered.
