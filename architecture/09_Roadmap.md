# Roadmap

## Purpose

This roadmap describes the long-term evolution of The Coherence Project.

Its purpose is to provide direction rather than deadlines.

The roadmap represents a vision of progressive capability, not a fixed implementation schedule.

---

# Guiding Principle

The project evolves through learning.

Each new capability should emerge from demonstrated need rather than anticipation.

The roadmap should be reviewed periodically as the project grows.

---

# Phase 1 — Foundation

## Objective

Establish a solid foundation for the project.

## Deliverables

- Documentation.
- Website.
- Bilingual structure.
- Initial design system.
- Initial technical architecture.
- Publication workflow.

## Success Criteria

The project can be maintained consistently with low operational effort.

---

# Phase 2 — Knowledge Base

## Objective

Build the project's core body of knowledge.

## Deliverables

- Essay I
- Essay II
- Essay III
- Supporting articles.
- The Coherence Journal.
- Research notes.

## Success Criteria

The project contains a coherent and interconnected knowledge base.

---

# Phase 3 — Educational Platform

## Objective

Make the ideas more accessible.

## Potential Deliverables

- Podcast.
- Recorded talks.
- Workshops.
- Practical guides.
- Interviews.

## Success Criteria

People can understand the ideas through multiple learning formats.

---

# Phase 4 — Applied Knowledge

## Objective

Demonstrate Organizational Coherence through practical application.

## Potential Deliverables

- Case studies.
- Organizational analyses.
- Business experiments.
- Practical frameworks.
- Decision tools.

## Success Criteria

The theory consistently explains and improves real-world situations.

---

# Phase 5 — Community

## Objective

Create meaningful dialogue around the work.

## Potential Deliverables

- Newsletter.
- Reader feedback.
- Discussion spaces.
- Collaborative research.
- Events.

## Success Criteria

The project becomes a place where ideas continue evolving through thoughtful interaction.

---

# Phase 6 — Learning Ecosystem

## Objective

Expand beyond publications.

## Potential Deliverables

- Courses.
- Educational programs.
- Learning pathways.
- Speaking engagements.
- Mentoring.
- Certification (if appropriate).

## Success Criteria

The project enables structured learning experiences without compromising its research integrity.

---

# Continuous Activities

The following activities exist across every phase:

- Research.
- Writing.
- Revision.
- Translation.
- Reading.
- Observation.
- Experimentation.

These activities never finish.

---

# Success Measures

Progress should be evaluated by questions such as:

- Is the knowledge becoming clearer?
- Is the project becoming more coherent?
- Are readers able to apply the ideas?
- Is each new publication strengthening the whole?

Progress is measured by accumulated understanding rather than publication volume.

---

# Roadmap Philosophy

This roadmap is directional.

It should guide decisions without restricting discovery.

Unexpected opportunities may change the sequence, but they should remain aligned with the project's principles.

The roadmap exists to preserve coherence while allowing evolution.
