# Product Vision

## Purpose

The Coherence Project is an open research initiative dedicated to developing, documenting and disseminating the principles of Organizational Coherence.

It combines theoretical research, practical experimentation and public education into a single knowledge platform.

Rather than presenting a finished framework, the project documents the continuous evolution of ideas, evidence and applications.

---

## Mission

To help leaders build organizations capable of consistently delivering the promises they make to the market.

---

## Vision

To become a globally recognized reference on Organizational Coherence, connecting academic rigor with practical business application.

---

## Problem Statement

Organizations often focus on growth, innovation and strategic planning while overlooking a more fundamental question:

"Is the organization actually capable of delivering the promise it makes?"

The absence of coherence between promise and capability leads to operational friction, declining competitiveness and strategic inconsistency.

The Coherence Project exists to explore, explain and address this challenge.

---

## Core Value Proposition

The project provides a structured body of knowledge that combines:

- Original theoretical essays
- Accessible educational articles
- Real-world experimentation
- Continuous research
- Practical frameworks
- Public documentation

---

## Product Principles

- Knowledge before promotion.
- Clarity before complexity.
- Evidence before opinion.
- Practice validates theory.
- Long-form thinking over short-form trends.
- Open knowledge whenever possible.
- Continuous evolution instead of static publication.

---

## Success Metrics

The project's success is measured by long-term intellectual impact rather than short-term audience metrics.

Examples include:

- Published essays
- Research citations
- Meaningful discussions generated
- Practical applications
- International recognition
- Invitations to teach, write or speak

---

## Non-Goals

The project is not intended to become:

- A personal blog.
- A news website.
- A content marketing platform.
- A productivity channel.
- A social media business.

Its purpose is to build a lasting body of knowledge.

---

## Long-Term Vision

The Coherence Project should evolve into an international knowledge platform where theory, research, experimentation and education reinforce one another, creating a continuously expanding body of work on Organizational Coherence.
