# Content Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines how knowledge is created, refined, published and connected throughout The Coherence Project.

The objective is not to maximize content production.

The objective is to maximize understanding.

---

# Knowledge Hierarchy

Not every piece of content has the same *purpose*, even when it belongs to the same domain.

This document applies a purpose-based lens — Primary, Educational and Experimental — over the knowledge domains already defined in **02_Information_Architecture**. It does not introduce new content types; it explains why each domain exists.

Each layer reinforces the others.

---

# Primary Knowledge

Primary knowledge represents original intellectual work.

It defines the project's ideas rather than explaining existing ones.

Domains: Essays, Book (future).

Characteristics:

- Evergreen
- Long-form
- Theory-driven
- Carefully revised
- Published infrequently

---

# Educational Knowledge

Educational content makes the theory accessible.

It should simplify ideas without oversimplifying them.

Domains: Articles, Podcast.

Characteristics:

- Accessible
- Practical
- Contextual
- Audience-oriented

---

# Experimental Knowledge

Experimental knowledge documents the interaction between theory and reality.

Its purpose is learning rather than storytelling.

Domain: The Coherence Journal.

Characteristics:

- Honest
- Transparent
- Iterative
- Evidence-based

Entries should document both successful and unsuccessful outcomes.

---

# Supporting Knowledge

Research does not sit inside a single layer above — it feeds all three.

Reading notes, references and open questions may evolve into primary, educational or experimental content over time (see 02_Information_Architecture → Content Relationships).

---

## Editorial Assets and Visual Narrative

Diagrams and figures are part of the editorial narrative, not decorative extras. They support the argument, clarify relationships and make complex ideas easier to follow.

Every figure should include a caption and, when appropriate, a reference in the surrounding text. This ensures readers can connect visual material to the argument and maintain narrative flow.

Diagrams belong to the content itself; they should be treated as first-class editorial assets rather than optional decorations.

---

# Publishing Philosophy

Publishing is not an end.

Publishing is a mechanism for improving ideas through public dialogue.

Quality takes precedence over frequency.

---

# Editorial Workflow

Ideas evolve through multiple stages.

Research

↓

Notes

↓

Frameworks

↓

Essays

↓

Articles

↓

Journal Applications

↓

Book

Knowledge should become more refined as it moves through the system.

> **Note:** this is the *idea-maturation* pipeline (how a single idea deepens). It is distinct from the Content Relationships diagram in 02_Information_Architecture, which shows *reader navigation* between published formats. Keep both, but don't merge them — they answer different questions.

---

# Content Relationships

Each publication should naturally guide readers toward deeper understanding.

For example:

Article

↓

Related Essay

↓

Journal Entry

↓

Additional Research

The objective is exploration, not retention.

---

# Writing Principles

Every publication should strive to be:

- Clear
- Honest
- Practical
- Evidence-informed
- Respectful of the reader's time

Complexity should emerge from the subject, never from the writing.

---

# Multilingual Publishing

English and Portuguese are maintained independently.

Translations should preserve ideas rather than sentence structure.

Whenever appropriate, examples and references may be adapted for each audience.

---

# Success Criteria

Successful content:

- Improves understanding.
- Generates thoughtful discussion.
- Remains valuable over time.
- Encourages deeper exploration.
- Strengthens the credibility of the project.

Popularity alone is not considered success.

---

# Long-Term Goal

The Coherence Project should become a living body of knowledge where every publication contributes to a coherent understanding of Organizational Coherence.

Every new piece of content should make the entire project more valuable.
